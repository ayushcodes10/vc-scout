Risks, conflicts and open questions

Risks

  • The headline traction figure ('1,000+ support teams') appears only on the company's own pages with no independent corroboration. [S3] [S5]
  • The 67% conversation-handling metric comes from a single unverified customer testimonial and cannot be generalized. [S3]
  • The Show HN launch received minimal community engagement (1 point, 0 comments), indicating little external validation or attention. [S1]

Open questions

These are gaps in the research. None of them is a finding about the company.

  • Who founded or works at the company, and what is their background?
  • What is the size of the customer support software market or the company's target market share?
  • What are actual, independently verified customer counts, revenue, or usage metrics?
  • Are there any known technical limitations, outages, or failure cases of the AI agent?
  • What underlying AI models or technology power Zoona?

What would change our mind

  • Independent verification of customer count and usage metrics (e.g., third-party case studies or reviews) beyond the company's own site
  • Founder/team background information establishing domain expertise in customer support or AI agent workflows
  • Evidence of measurable ROI (time or cost savings) from an independent or corroborated source rather than a single testimonial
