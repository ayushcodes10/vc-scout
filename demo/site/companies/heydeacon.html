Product in plain language
HeyDeacon (Deacon) is an AI customer support agent for SaaS founders: it answers user questions, onboards users, and surfaces feedback/knowledge gaps by ingesting documentation, website content and how-to videos. It installs via a one-line script, scores its own answers for validity against the knowledge base, and captures visitor details/escalates when it cannot answer. Pricing is usage-based with a free tier and Starter ($40/mo)/Growth ($120/mo) paid tiers.

Investment view

Team

No source provides any information on HeyDeacon's founders, employees, or their backgrounds, so team composition and earned insight cannot be assessed. Open question

Product

Deacon is an AI support agent that ingests docs/URLs/videos, installs via one line of script, answers customer questions, scores its own answers against the knowledge base rather than guessing, and escalates unanswered questions by capturing visitor details for a human reply. Pricing is usage-based with a free tier and two paid tiers ($40/$120 per month). [S2] [S3]

Market

The company positions itself against other AI customer support tools such as Fin and Decagon, targeting SaaS founders, but no market sizing or demand data is present. [S2]

Competition

  • The company explicitly names Fin and Decagon as competitors it differentiates itself from. [S2]

Risks, conflicts and open questions

Risks

  • Traction claims (NPS/revenue/conversion improvements, a switch from Intercom/Fin) rest solely on unattributed, undated homepage testimonials with no independent verification. [S2]
  • The Show HN launch received minimal community engagement (4 points, 1 comment), indicating limited external attention so far. [S1]
  • No information exists on funding or financial runway. Open question

Open questions

These are gaps in the research. None of them is a finding about the company.

  • Who founded or works at HeyDeacon, and what is their background?
  • How many customers, users, or paying accounts does HeyDeacon have?
  • What is the size of the market for AI customer support tools for SaaS founders?
  • What are the company's funding status or financial runway?

What would change our mind

  • Verified, named customer case studies with measurable before/after support metrics (e.g., resolution time, revenue impact) beyond homepage testimonials
  • Founder background and team composition evidence showing domain expertise in customer support or SaaS operations
  • Evidence of a repeatable distribution channel or paid customer count beyond a single low-engagement Show HN post
